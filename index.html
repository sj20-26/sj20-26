My Instructional Design Approach

Start with the learner. Instruction should be designed around what the learner needs to accomplish, not simply the information that needs to be presented.

Make learning practical. Realistic situations, decisions, and problems give learners opportunities to apply what they have learned.

Use feedback to support learning. Effective feedback helps learners understand what they did, why it was or was not correct, and what they should do next.
